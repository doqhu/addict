### Features

* a Russian word gets an offline translation into the chosen language — WikDict ru-en, ru-es and ru-ca (2_2026-06, pinned) are imported with addict import, each into its own index, with headwords keyed without stress marks (U+0301, U+0300 and the precomposed ѐ ѝ Ѐ Ѝ; headwords that become one are merged); a picked word with a Cyrillic letter is looked up only in the Russian index of the language on the switch, as written, in lowercase and with ё and е taken as one letter, and shows its translations under "&lt;Language&gt;, for the word as a whole:", or "No Russian-&lt;Language&gt; dictionary is imported."; nothing is sent online for it, neither the word nor its sentence; Latin-script words, the ten other indexes and the switch are unchanged ([ba4901e](https://github.com/doqhu/addict/commit/ba4901e84d0d87418e75ba99c919270db1d7b3e6))
